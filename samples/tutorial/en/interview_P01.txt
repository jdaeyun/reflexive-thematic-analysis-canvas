I: Thanks for doing this. To start, how have you been feeling about night shifts lately?
P01: Honestly... they hit me harder every year. When I was younger I'd go shopping straight after a night. Now I get home and collapse into bed.
I: So you've noticed changes in your body.
P01: Yes. Around four in the morning is the worst. My head goes foggy, and I've started double-checking every drip calculation. I'm scared of getting one wrong.
I: Do things happen at that time of night?
P01: Patients nearly always deteriorate in the early hours. It's strange, but they do. There's a doctor on call, but they cover other wards too, so they can't come straight away. The first call is ours to make.
I: The first call is yours.
P01: At night it's two nurses for thirty beds. Whether someone will make it to the morning — you have to judge that with your own eyes. In the daytime there's always someone around.
I: What goes through your mind at those moments?
P01: "If I miss it, nobody will notice." At night I'm the last line of defence. Maybe that sounds dramatic.
I: I don't think it does. Is there anything that helps you at times like that?
P01: The person I'm working with. The night staff on our ward get on really well. Someone saying "go and take your break, I'll keep an eye on things" makes all the difference.
P01: And snacks. Nobody knows who started it, but there's an unwritten rule that night staff bring something in (laughs). Honestly, it keeps me going.
I: Snacks.
P01: Silly, isn't it? But when someone hands you something at two in the morning and says "have this", you feel you're not on your own.
I: How about your family?
P01: My husband is pretty understanding. But when the kids were little it was hard. You come home after a night and of course they want you to play. They'd see me asleep and say "Mummy is always sleeping".
P01: That still stings a bit.
I: Is there anything else about nights you'd like your workplace to hear?
P01: I don't think the day staff see how much happens at night. At handover you say "nothing to report" and that's the end of it. Even when a lot went on.
I: It all gets folded into "nothing to report".
P01: Exactly. Mind you, I say it myself (laughs).
