Thematic Analysis Canvas

A browser-only workspace for analysing interview transcripts and open-ended survey answers with reflexive thematic analysis (Braun & Clarke), following its six phases. Nothing to install and no account. Your data never leaves your computer, and no AI is used.

Start with the 101 (about 45 min) Open the app Guide

Why this app

Built for reflexive thematic analysis

General-purpose QDA software is powerful, but it quietly pulls you towards frequency counts, inter-coder agreement and fixed codebooks. This app is shaped around the way reflexive thematic analysis actually works.

🔒Your data stays with you

Documents are stored only in your browser (IndexedDB) and are never uploaded. Easy to explain to an ethics committee.

✍️No AI

Coding, interpreting and writing are all done by you. No language model reads your data.

#Not decided by numbers

Prevalence counts are hidden by default and agreement is never calculated: what matters is decided by interpretation, not frequency.

📓Reflexivity on record

Every interpretive change goes into an audit trail automatically, and the reflexive journal is one keystroke away. Both become an appendix.

📄All the way to the manuscript

Phase 6 starts with writing. Export the analytic narrative, a thematic map and tables to Word (.docx).

🧭Mapped to the RTARG

Every item of the Reflexive Thematic Analysis Reporting Guidelines (Braun & Clarke, 2024) is mapped to what the app does.

How it works

Four tabs, six phases, back and forth

The phases are not a one-way street. Because you will move back to coding while reviewing themes, the phases are tracked as a status on each theme rather than as separate screens.

Tab 1

Familiarise & Code

Phases 1–2. Import transcripts and survey answers, read, make notes and code. Full-text search, speakers and extract ranges live here too.

Tab 2

Themes

Phases 3–5. Gather codes into themes, review them against the data, define and name them, and map how they relate.

Tab 3

Audit & Journal

What changed, when and why, next to your reflexive journal, with the theme structure saved at each entry.

Tab 4

Writing up

Phase 6. Tell the analytic story theme by theme, inserting extracts as evidence. Tables and the appendix support it.

Who it is for

Including your first qualitative project

  • Students analysing interviews or open-ended answers for a dissertation or thesis
  • Supervisors reading a student's analysis as a critical friend
  • Researchers in nursing, psychology, education, social work, sociology and beyond who use reflexive TA
  • Projects that cannot use paid software or cannot move data off a computer

Limits

Good to know before you start

  • Text only (.txt, .docx, .doc, .csv, and REFI-QDA .qdpx). No audio, video, images or PDF.
  • No codebook approaches (template or framework analysis) and no inter-coder agreement.
  • No simultaneous editing of one project by several people.
  • Your data lives only in the browser you use: back up regularly.
  • Made for desktop browsers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).

Get started

Three ways in

🌐 Use it in your browser

Open it straight from this site. Even online, nothing you import is sent anywhere.

💾 Download it

Walk through all six phases with a small fictional dataset in about 45 minutes.
