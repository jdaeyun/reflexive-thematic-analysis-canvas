Approach: Reflexive thematic analysis (Braun & Clarke)
Coding is interpretive, and your subjectivity is a resource, not a bias to remove. For that reason the app does not calculate inter-coder agreement: use the reflexive journal and team discussion to make your analytic process transparent.

Everything is stored in this browser only (IndexedDB). Use Backup regularly: clearing site data deletes the project.
